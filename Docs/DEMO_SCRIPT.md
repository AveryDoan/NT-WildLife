# Two-minute demo script

Setup (before you go on): simulator or iPhone, Profile > **Demo mode ON**. Have a photo ready that was taken in the last 72 hours (on the simulator, drag one into Photos first and check its date).

1. **0:00 Today** (10 s). "TrailField helps Territorians photograph insects safely and learn what they are." Point at the Demo tag, the streak, the safety banner.
2. **0:10 Capture** (15 s). Tap Capture. "Look, don't touch" banner first. Choose from photos.
3. **0:25 Identify** (30 s). Show "Taken X minutes ago, this can earn points". If a Suggestion appears: "This is a hint from the phone, with its confidence, and I decide." Pick **Cathedral termite or Common crow** (not yet found in demo mode), then Save.
4. **0:55 Result** (20 s). Show the points breakdown (species, first find, streak) and the Unverified tag: "We only publish facts we've checked, otherwise it says so."
5. **1:15 Field Guide** (15 s). The new card is filled in, progress rings, the To identify shelf.
6. **1:30 Map** (15 s). "Locations are rounded to about 1 km, and rare species like Leichhardt's grasshopper to about 11 km."
7. **1:45 Ranks** (10 s). "Sample players only; a real server would plug in behind one protocol."
8. **1:55 Close.** "Private by design: nickname only, no people in photos, everything on the phone, Delete all my data in Profile."

If asked what's missing: open Docs/LIMITATIONS.md. Be upfront that only 5 species are seeded and 2 verified.
